# Daisy 1.0.8.37

- **What's New in Daisy.** After this update a short window shows the Daisy key in three animated steps: tap for a note that goes straight into its project, hold and say what to do, hold without a word to rewrite the selection. It shows your own key, and Help → What's New in Daisy opens it again. If you have no Daisy key yet, it says so and takes you to Settings.
