# Daisy 1.0.7.64

- **Live dictation preview.** With the Apple engine and a concrete language selected, a caption next to the widget shows your words as you speak — word by word, before the final paste.
- **Choose your meeting apps.** Settings now list every app Daisy can watch for calls, each with its own toggle — including Telegram.
- **Stop & delete.** The widget menu can now discard a recording in one step: stop, delete the audio and the note, no leftovers.
- **Screenshots live in the session.** Captured screens get their own section in the session view, and a screenshot note's pill has a trash button to toss a bad take instantly.
- **Storage at a glance.** A new storage section shows how much space recordings, models and caches take — and where.
- **Voice profile understands languages.** The dictation corpus is now tracked per language, and "Rewrite in my voice" picks examples matching the language you're writing in.
- **Better summaries when things go wrong.** Meeting summaries now cite evidence from the transcript, and capture problems are diagnosed more precisely — including virtual microphones.
- **MCP upgrades.** The built-in MCP server speaks Streamable HTTP, ships tool annotations, and connects to Cursor out of the box.
- Updated the on-device audio stack (FluidAudio 0.15.6) with fixes for Intel Macs.
