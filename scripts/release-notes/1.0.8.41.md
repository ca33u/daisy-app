# Daisy 1.0.8.41

- **A tap on the Daisy key fixes the keyboard layout** of the selection or the word you just typed. Notes are a hold now: hold and say “note to project Work: …”, let go, and it's there.
- **“…and summarize” at the end of a note** saves it with a summary.
- **“Make a prompt for…”** writes the prompt the way the tool you name expects it: ChatGPT, Midjourney, Gemini, FLUX, Ideogram, Veo, Runway, Kling, or a coding agent.
- **“Note only” and “Rewrite in my voice” are no longer separate shortcuts.** Both live on the Daisy key: hold and say “note…”, or hold without a word over selected text.
- **“What's New in Daisy”** can be opened again from Settings → About, next to the update controls.
