# Daisy 1.0.8.54

- **Your voice is recorded when you're in a call on AirPods.** When the call app was using AirPods or another Bluetooth headset for the call, Daisy recorded the Mac's built-in microphone, which hears you faintly, and your side of the meeting could be missing from the transcript. Now, if no microphone is chosen in Settings or the chosen one isn't connected, Daisy records the headset microphone the call app is using: at the start of a recording, and about ten seconds after a call begins while it records. About half a minute after the call app lets go of the headset, Daisy goes back to the microphone it would otherwise use, so the headset doesn't stay in call mode because of Daisy (with the Mac's lid closed and no other microphone it stays on the headset, since the built-in microphone records nothing then). If no audio at all comes from the headset, Daisy moves off it too when there is another microphone to use. A connected microphone chosen in Settings is used as before
- **The end of the welcome tour can no longer close Daisy.** On one Mac with macOS 26 Daisy quit while the flower flew to the widget at the end of the tour. The flight is now drawn differently
