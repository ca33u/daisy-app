# Daisy 1.0.8.6

- **The permission check works in Bluetooth headphones too.** Daisy checks that it may record system sound by listening for a tone nobody hears. It now plays that tone on the Mac's own output, so headphones in a call can no longer make the check read "not allowed".
- The update notice drops its trailing period.

Also in this build, from 1.0.8.5: the other side is recorded in Bluetooth headphones; Daisy asks for System Audio Recording once, in its own window; if the way Daisy listens goes quiet, it tries the other one in the same recording; a shorter warning when the other side can't be heard.
