# Daisy 1.0.7.70

- **Dictation is fast again.** The optional "polish in my voice" rewrite has been removed: it added about seven seconds between letting go of the key and seeing your text, and sent every dictation to your summary provider. Your style profile is still there and still learned from your dictation — it just applies where it belongs, to follow-ups and to the rewrite shortcut, rather than to text that is already in your own words.
- **Everything you can click now looks like it.** The sidebar's own rows and the Record button light up under the pointer, like Settings and About already did.
