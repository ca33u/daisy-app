# Daisy 1.0.8.53

- **With sync off, Daisy puts nothing of yours in iCloud.** Settings said so, yet three things ran whether «Sync with the iPhone through iCloud» was on or off: the project list, with each project’s notes and tracker settings, and the dictation vocabulary went to iCloud’s key-value storage; saved keys and tokens were iCloud Keychain items, carried to your other devices when iCloud Keychain is on for this Mac; and the Mac announced itself by name on the local network for recordings from Daisy for iPhone. All three now wait for the switch. With sync off the project list and the vocabulary stay on this Mac, keys and tokens are saved so that iCloud Keychain doesn’t carry them, and the Mac doesn’t listen on the local network. With sync on everything works as before. If you use Daisy for iPhone or a second Mac: with sync off here, a key you save, a project you change and a word you add no longer reach them, and theirs don’t reach this Mac. Turn sync on to have them travel again
- **Updating with sync off keeps your keys and projects.** At the first launch Daisy makes its own copy on this Mac of the keys and the project list it finds in iCloud, so nothing has to be entered again. What earlier versions put in iCloud stays there until you remove it with «Delete my data from iCloud…»
- **«Delete my data from iCloud…» takes out keys and vocabulary too.** With sync off, the button now removes from iCloud the sessions Daisy synced, the dictation vocabulary and the keys and tokens Daisy keeps in iCloud Keychain. Everything on this Mac stays. The project list stays in iCloud: Daisy for iPhone keeps it nowhere else. After that Daisy for iPhone has no keys until you turn sync on again on a Mac that has them, and Daisy on another Mac loses them too if it has sync on or is an older version. With sync on the button removes the sessions only, as before, and the dialog says how to remove the rest: turn sync off first
