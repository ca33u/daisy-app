# Daisy 1.0.8.50

- **The layout key switches whatever you give it.** The shortcut that fixes the keyboard layout now switches the last word or the selection on every press: «сайт» becomes «cfqn», and an abbreviation, a name or a password typed in an ordinary field goes over too. It used to refuse when the word already read as a real one; that check now belongs only to fixing the layout as you type. Press again and the word, or a short selection, is back as it was. The last word is found even with spaces typed after it, commas and full stops survive the trip both ways, and a word longer than 20 characters is no longer cut off
- **Voice notes are summarized as your own notes.** A voice note recorded on the Mac now gets a summary that keeps your thought in your words, not minutes of a meeting with one participant, and no follow-up email. They are summarized automatically only when your summary provider runs on this Mac. To let a cloud provider do it, turn on Settings → Shortcuts → Daisy key → Summarize voice notes: it covers the notes you record from then on, and only the provider chosen when you turned it on. Voice notes are also marked in their files, so other apps that read your Daisy folder can tell a voice note from other notes and meetings
- **Settings and About close on a click outside.** Click the window around the sheet and it closes, the way × and Escape close it. Dragging the window, or a selection that began inside the sheet, leaves it open
