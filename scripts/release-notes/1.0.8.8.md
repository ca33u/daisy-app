# Daisy 1.0.8.8

- **No more gaps in the other side's transcript.** When speech and something repetitive — a song's chorus, a jingle — landed in the same stretch, the final transcript could drop several seconds of clear speech. Daisy now goes back over any audible stretch that came out without text and transcribes it again.
- **Ready for the next recording the moment you press Stop.** The widget no longer shows a separate "summary" state: summaries are made in the background, and the flower is ready to record again straight away.
