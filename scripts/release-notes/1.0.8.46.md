# Daisy 1.0.8.46

- **Dictate while a meeting records.** The dictation key now works during a recording: the text goes where your cursor is, and the words you dictated stay out of the meeting’s transcript and summary.
- **See where a meeting is after Stop.** The meeting card shows each step — transcript with its percent, voices, names and terms, screenshot text, summary. The Summary button waits until there is a transcript, and meetings recorded without live transcription now get their summary automatically.
- **Dictation lands once.** Text no longer appears twice in apps like Telegram, apps where direct insertion never works are typed into straight away, a silent hold no longer pastes stray words, and you get your own soft sound when you start and when the text lands.
- **Layout fix without selecting.** The layout-fix shortcut now fixes the last word before the cursor and switches the layout; press again within five seconds to undo. Nearly any key can be a shortcut, even a single ⌥ — Daisy warns if it’s already in use instead of refusing.
- **Settings in their own window.** Settings and About open over the main window with their sections always in view; ⌘, opens them from anywhere.
- **Ollama starts by itself.** If Ollama is installed but not running, Daisy launches it in the background before saying it’s off. Short, clearer messages in the widget bubbles too.
