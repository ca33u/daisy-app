# Daisy 1.0.8.15

- **Next steps from your iPhone stay put.** Steps you sent to Calendar, Mail or Reminders on the phone keep their marks when your Mac edits or rewrites that summary.
- **Voices your iPhone told apart are kept.** The Mac no longer re-splits a recording the phone already listened to.
- **Dictation's tabs** use the same toolbar control as Settings, so they don't disappear in a narrow window.
- **A smoother start** when Screenshot notes are on: the first look at your screenshots folder no longer holds up the app.
