# Daisy 1.0.7.68

- **Import your own recordings.** Drop audio files (m4a, mp3, wav, aiff, aac, flac) or videos (mp4, mov, m4v — only the sound is kept) onto the Library, or open them with Daisy from Finder or the Dock. Each file becomes a recording with the file's own date and name; a dropped folder becomes a project. Choose copy or move, and transcribe now, later, or tonight at a time you pick — the queue lives on disk, waits for any live recording to finish, and survives sleep and quitting.
- **Notes and recordings are one Library.** Instead of two sidebar entries there is a single Library with an All / Recordings / Notes filter — same folders, tags and files, told apart by one chip.
- **Settings and About sit in the sidebar's lower group** with the same typography and icons as the rows above them.
- **Widget menu tidied.** The Hide submenu is gone; its three options sit directly in the menu, without the odd white highlight.
