# Daisy 1.0.8.32

- **Crash reports, only when you send one.** If Daisy closes unexpectedly, the next launch asks whether to send a report to the developer and shows the whole report first: where it failed, the app and macOS versions and the Mac model — never your meetings, names, files or devices. Nothing is sent without your answer; About → Crash reports can turn the question off.
- **A pause no longer pulls the two voices apart.** Recordings with pauses keep your voice and the others' lined up in exports.
- **New widget colours:** a sage centre at rest, orange for a meeting, lilac for dictation, blue for a voice note, red for an error; when paused the centre stays orange and the petals turn grey.
- **GPT-6.1 Sol is the default OpenAI model** — faster and cheaper than GPT-5.6 Terra, which stays in the list. Token costs now count OpenAI's prompt-cache writes at their own price.
