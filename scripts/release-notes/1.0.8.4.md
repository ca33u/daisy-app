# Daisy 1.0.8.4

- **"Can't hear the other side" now clears itself.** If Daisy warned that it couldn't hear the other side — sound going to AirPods, say — and you switched to the speakers, the warning stayed up until the end of the meeting although the other side was being recorded again. Now it comes down the moment they can be heard, with a short "The other side can be heard again".
- **The flower answers your voice more clearly.** While recording, the petals move over twice the range they did.
- **A slightly larger menu-bar icon.**

Also in this build, from 1.0.8.3:

- The menu-bar flower is smaller inside its disc and rests with a white centre; while recording the centre is orange, as on the iPhone and the watch.
- "Daisy" in dictation is written as Daisy when it is heard as «Дейзи» or «Дэйзи».
- Loop filter, fixed: a real short reply right next to a repeated phrase is no longer thrown away with it.
- Sessions from Daisy for iPhone keep their kind when you move them between folders.
