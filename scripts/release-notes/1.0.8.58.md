# Daisy 1.0.8.58

- **Daisy Subscription: summaries without your own API key.** A new provider in Settings → AI. Summaries and templates, the brief before a meeting and the morning brief, the second pass over the transcript, speaker names, «What did I miss?», rewrites in your voice, «My style» and Daisy key commands run on Claude through Daisy’s server; the server passes the text to Anthropic and keeps none of it. Meeting plan analysis still needs OpenAI. Recording, transcription and every provider you already use work as before, with no account
- **It needs a Daisy account.** Sign in with a code sent to your email or with Google. Before the first sign-in Daisy asks whether text may go through its server, and sends nothing without a yes. Names and contacts are replaced before sending only if «Protect sensitive data before cloud AI» is on in Settings. The sign-in stays on this Mac and isn’t synced through iCloud. You can sign out or delete the account in the same place; deleting it doesn’t cancel a paid subscription. Sign in with Apple isn’t on the Mac yet
- **Three meeting summaries are free for every account.** They use Daisy’s standard summary, without your templates. The other tasks need the subscription
- **$7.99 a month or $59.99 a year, bought from Settings.** Daisy opens the checkout in your browser, the payment goes through Whop, and Settings shows the subscription once the payment is confirmed. It belongs to your account, not to this Mac: sign in on another Mac and it works there. There are daily and monthly limits on use; when one is reached, the summary says so and the transcript is kept. Not yet tried against the live server: neither a sign-in from the app, nor a summary, nor a real payment
- **Plan analysis through Codex only on a ChatGPT account.** If Codex is signed in with an API key, the meeting’s plan no longer goes out on that key, as summaries already didn’t
- **«Delete audio files» in Settings says what it deletes.** The audio of transcribed recordings; imported audio and recordings without a transcript stay
