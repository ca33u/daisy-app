# Daisy 1.0.8.61

- **«What’s New» is easier to leave.** A round × on the photograph, top right, and «Not now» is a button of its own right under «Subscribe»; before, it was a line of small text under the fine print. Esc still closes the window
- **One subscription for Mac and iPhone.** A new row in «What’s New»: the subscription belongs to your Daisy account, so it works on any Mac you sign in on, and when Daisy for iPhone comes out it will work there too
- **The texts of «What’s New» are proofread.** Signed in, the free-summaries line now says that the three need «Write summaries on the Daisy Subscription» turned on, while it is off: signing in alone writes nothing. The renewal line says where to cancel in one go
- If you saw «What’s New» on 1.0.8.60, it won’t open by itself again: Help → «What’s New in Daisy» shows the new one
- Still not tried live: a real payment, a summary written on the subscription, and «Fix dictation by context» on a real request
