# Daisy 1.0.8.48

- **Translate by voice with the Daisy key.** Say the language as part of the request: «translate into Portuguese: see you tomorrow», «how do you say good afternoon in Japanese», «tell Pete in Spanish that I’m running late». Over a selection in a text field, «translate into English» replaces it in place, with Undo; in text you can’t edit, the translation goes to the card. Your summary model translates (a cloud one asks first, as always); without one, Daisy uses Apple’s translation on this Mac (macOS 26 and later). Brazilian or European Portuguese, American or British English, simplified or traditional Chinese — just say which.
- **Summaries survive a model’s slip.** One section or point the model wrote incompletely no longer costs the whole summary: Claude’s answer is now held to the summary’s format, and for every provider a broken piece is mended or left out instead. If a summary still fails, the message says once what went wrong.
- **A model’s thinking never lands in your text.** When a reasoning model writes out its thoughts before the answer, Daisy takes them off before anything is typed. A Daisy-key result much longer than you asked for goes to the card, not into the field.
- **Parakeet and the speaker models mend themselves after an interrupted download.** If the Mac slept or the network dropped while one was downloading, Daisy now fetches only the missing files when it loads, instead of failing until the cache is cleared by hand.
- **Your library, readable by other apps.** Daisy now describes its meetings and projects in a small file next to them, so an app like Flow can show your meetings inside its projects, and links like daisy://session/… open a meeting in Daisy. Renaming a project keeps its subprojects and its place, and a project renamed on another Mac no longer sends its meetings to Inbox.
