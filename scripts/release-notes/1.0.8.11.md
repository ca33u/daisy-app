# Daisy 1.0.8.11

Everything since 1.0.8.2:

- **Bluetooth headphones no longer silence the other side.** On macOS 14.4 and later Daisy records system audio a new way that works in call mode too. It asks once for the System Audio Recording permission, on its own screen and not in the middle of a call; if that capture ever stays silent, the recording switches back to the old way on the spot.
- **Fewer gaps in the other side's speech.** Audible stretches the final transcript missed are read again.
- **A recording cut short by the next one gets its final transcript and summary**, in the same recording. Holding the dictation key right after a meeting no longer interrupts the meeting at all.
- **Echo from the speakers is cut out of your own lines**, and switching microphones mid-recording no longer shifts what you say after it.
- **The "can't hear the other side" warning goes away by itself** as soon as the sound is back.
- **Audio diagnostics during a recording** show the recording's own levels and devices, including headphones connected or removed mid-recording. Permissions have a System Audio Recording row.
- **Install a downloaded update from the menu:** "Check for Updates" becomes "Install Daisy … and Restart".
- **The widget** is ready for the next recording the moment you press Stop, its petals follow your voice more visibly, and its resting centre is white. The menu-bar icon is a little larger. Headings use a rounded font.
- **Voice Memos import has moved to Daisy for iPhone.** The Mac no longer scans the Voice Memos folder.
