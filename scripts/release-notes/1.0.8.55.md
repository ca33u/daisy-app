# Daisy 1.0.8.55

- **⌘V after a dictation pastes what you copied.** When Daisy found no text field to type into, it pasted the dictation through the clipboard and put your clipboard back four seconds later, so a ⌘V in between pasted the dictation again. Now, when you press ⌘V yourself, Daisy puts back what you had copied first, and that is what gets pasted. The same goes for the other texts Daisy pastes through the clipboard. A ⌘V within the first 0.4 seconds after Daisy’s paste, a paste from the Edit menu, a paste in a password field and a clipboard that was empty work as before. «Paste my last dictation» still pastes the dictation
- **A recording doesn’t start without a place to save it.** If Daisy couldn’t create the recording’s folder, for example on a drive that had become read-only, the recording went on and nothing was saved, and at the end it looked finished. Now Daisy checks at the start that the folder takes files. If the folder you picked doesn’t, the recording goes to Daisy’s own folder and Daisy says so. If there is nowhere to save at all, a meeting or voice note doesn’t start and Daisy says why. A dictation starts anyway, since its text goes to the cursor
- **Short dictations with «Don't record audio» are no longer lost.** With no audio saved, a dictation under ten seconds was dropped as too short if no live text had appeared. And if no sound at all reached Daisy while you held the Daisy key over selected text for ten seconds or more, it could rewrite the selection; now it says it didn’t hear anything
- **Notion: recordings can go to your meeting notes.** In Settings, Connections, Notion, under the gear, «Parent type» is now «Where to», with a third choice: Meeting note. The summary and the transcript go into the Notes tab, without a link to the calendar event; a transcript too long for one note is cut, and the note says so. It needs a personal access token instead of an integration secret, and such a token acts as you in Notion. After you change «Where to», automatic sending is off until the connection test passes and you turn it on again
- **Notion and Claude get what they should.** A recording sent from the Library carried its whole file, with the summary a second time and the paths of screenshots; now it carries the transcript and what you said at marked moments. The summary’s outline goes along too. A long meeting sent to a page or database, more than 100 blocks in Notion, was refused; now it is sent in parts, and if Notion doesn’t take all of them, Daisy moves the incomplete page to Notion’s trash, or tells you that it is still there
