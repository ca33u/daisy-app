# Daisy 1.0.8.29

- **A new Daisy mark.** The app icon, the menu bar icon and the widget use the refreshed daisy, turned slightly to the left.
- **A livelier widget.** While Daisy prepares or stops a recording, the petals curve and spin clockwise, then ease to a stop; while recording they react to your voice as before.
- **No grey at rest.** The widget's petals keep their cream colour when Daisy is idle.
