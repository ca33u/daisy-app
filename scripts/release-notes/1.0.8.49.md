# Daisy 1.0.8.49

- **Dictation no longer waits for Parakeet after a launch.** If Parakeet is your dictation model and it’s already downloaded, Daisy now loads it when it starts, so the first dictation after an update or a restart doesn’t stall on «Loading the speech model». Press while it’s still loading, and the dictation waits for it. When the Mac runs critically short of memory, Daisy releases the model and loads it again from disk on the next dictation.
- **Grok for summaries.** Pick Grok in Settings → AI → Summary provider and paste an xAI API key. Grok 4.3 is the default, about $0.03 per hour of meeting; Grok 4.7 is there too, better and pricier. Daisy asks xAI not to store its replies, and after a summary Settings shows whether Zero Data Retention is on for your xAI team: without it, xAI keeps requests for 30 days to check for abuse.
- **Cursor: pick the model from Cursor’s list.** Next to the Model field, Choose lists the models your Cursor plan offers; you can still type any model id. Settings also says that unless Privacy Mode is on, Cursor may store your transcripts and train its models on them, and where to turn it on.
