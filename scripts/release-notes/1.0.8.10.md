# Daisy 1.0.8.10

- **Dictate right after a meeting.** Holding the dictation key while the last meeting is still being finished no longer cancels its final transcript and summary — the dictation runs alongside and pastes in seconds.
- **Echo from the speakers is cut out of your own lines.** Without headphones, the other side's words picked up by your microphone no longer end up in your line of the transcript; your own words stay.
- **Your lines stay in step with the other side.** Switching microphones mid-recording (say, taking off Bluetooth headphones) no longer shifts everything you say after it a few seconds early.
- **Fewer gaps in the other side's speech.** Speech the final pass dropped is re-read without your vocabulary hints, which were the cause on long stretches.
- **Audio diagnostics follow your devices during a recording** and name the microphone the recording is actually using.
- **Install the downloaded update from the menu.** Once an update is ready, "Check for Updates" becomes "Install Daisy … and Restart".
