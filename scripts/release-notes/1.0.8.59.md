# Daisy 1.0.8.59

- **A payment page you closed is no longer a dead end.** If you open the checkout for the Daisy Subscription and close the tab, Settings → AI now shows «Open the payment page again» and «Cancel» beside «Waiting for the payment…»; before, there was only the spinner for ten minutes. After Cancel, Daisy keeps checking until those ten minutes are up and shows a payment that went through; once the payment is confirmed, it won’t open a second checkout
- **Settings → AI in two blocks.** «Daisy Subscription» is always there, with a switch «Write summaries on the Daisy Subscription»: you can sign in and subscribe without first giving up the provider you use. «Summary provider» below it is your own provider (a key, an account, a local model). It stays as it was while the subscription writes, and switching the subscription off goes back to it. If you chose the subscription in 1.0.8.58, the provider you had before it wasn’t kept: «Summary provider» shows the default one. Switch the subscription off to pick yours again
- **A payment made from the block turns the switch on**, if Daisy sees it while «Waiting for the payment…» is showing. After Cancel, or once the ten minutes are up, the switch stays as it was, and Settings says that the subscription is active but summaries are still written by your provider
- **When Daisy talks to its server.** Signing in no longer needs the subscription chosen as the provider. Signed in, Daisy reads the account and the subscription’s status when you open Settings → AI, whether the switch is on or off, and asks for the status every three seconds for up to ten minutes after you open the payment page or come back from it. While the switch is on it also asks in the background, as before. Text goes through Daisy’s server only while the switch is on
- **Back from a payment that wasn’t completed, Settings shows the prices again**, not a wait for a payment. This works through the «Return to Daisy» button on the page that says so
- **«Help Daisy stay independent» in About.** It opens mydaisy.io/support-daisy, a page for a one-time payment of any amount, in place of Ko-fi
