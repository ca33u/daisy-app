# Daisy 1.0.8.42

- **A new first run.** Daisy introduces herself and teaches by doing: drag the widget where you like, dictate into a practice field, read a passage aloud, hold the Daisy key for a note, see what she makes of a meeting. Nine short steps, each one skippable. Already set up? Help → Take the tour again.
- **Setup questions moved to where they matter.** The calendar, the summary provider and a missing project are asked about once, at the widget, the moment you need them — not on the first run.
- **The widget remembers where you dragged it**, across launches and displays.
- **A demo meeting** (“Demo: landing mockups”) shows a transcript by speaker, a summary and tasks before you record a call of your own. It's marked “demo” and deletes like any note.
- **Style profile:** the words you dictate in the tour count toward it; at 300 words Daisy asks once whether to build it.
- **One standard speech model** for meetings and dictation on the first run (about 600 MB); the faster dictation engine stays in Settings.
