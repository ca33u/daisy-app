# Daisy 1.0.8.47

- **Settings inside the main window.** Settings and About open as a panel over the main window, the window behind softly blurred, like What’s New. Settings keep their sections on the left; About is a single page. Escape, ⌘W or × closes the panel.
- **Report a bug and beta updates are back in About.** Send a log report or switch on beta updates from About again. They also stay where they were in Settings.
- **A softer drop.** The dictation sound is now a soft, rounder water drop, and meetings use it too when recording starts and when the transcript is ready.
