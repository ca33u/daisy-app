# Daisy 1.0.8.47

Everything new since 1.0.8.32.

- **The Daisy key.** One key for notes and commands. Hold it and say «note to project Work: …» to save a note there, or «shorter», «translate to English», «write a reply…», «make a prompt for Midjourney…» to work on the selected text or at the cursor. Held in a text field, it does what you ask and puts the result at the cursor. A silent hold over a selection rewrites it in your voice, and a tap fixes the keyboard layout of the word you just typed. In a meeting, a hold marks the moment with your comment.
- **Dictate while a meeting records.** The text goes where your cursor is and stays out of the meeting's transcript and summary. Dictation lands once in apps like Telegram, starts without waiting for the meeting model, and has its own soft sound. You choose how long its history is kept.
- **Layout fix without selecting.** The shortcut fixes the last word before the cursor and switches the layout; press it again within five seconds to undo. Nearly any key can be a shortcut, even a single ⌥, and Daisy warns when a key is already in use.
- **A new first run.** A short tour teaches by doing: dictate into a practice field, hold the Daisy key for a note, and look at a demo meeting with a transcript by speaker, a summary and tasks. About → Take the tour again repeats it.
- **Settings in eight tabs, inside the main window.** General, Shortcuts, Meetings, Dictation, AI, Library, Connections and Privacy, with search at the top. Settings and About open as a panel over the main window. Report a bug and beta updates are in About.
- **See where a meeting is after Stop.** The meeting card shows each step with its progress, and the Summary button waits for the transcript. Meetings recorded without live transcription get their summary automatically. Starting a new recording never waits for the previous one to finish.
- **More ways to write summaries.** Ollama models download in one click, Ollama starts by itself, and summaries of long meetings finish. YandexGPT, GigaChat and DeepSeek join the providers, each on your own key. A slow cloud provider is never billed twice for one answer.
- **Your own templates** for follow-up letters and meeting notes, chosen by folder, a word in the title, or as the default.
- **Bluetooth headsets on long calls.** When AirPods switch into call mode and back, Daisy rebuilds the capture so the other side is still recorded.
- **Fixes.** Intel Macs no longer crash; the meeting model loads even without access to Documents; iCloud sync no longer overwrites an edit made on another device; imports skip files already in the Library; the widget remembers where you put it.
- **Help make Daisy better, if you want to.** Daisy asks once whether it may send a short daily count of which features ran and what failed. Numbers only, never text or names; Settings → Privacy shows exactly what is sent.
