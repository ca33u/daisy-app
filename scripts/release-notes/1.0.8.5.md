# Daisy 1.0.8.5

- **The other side is recorded in Bluetooth headphones.** When a call app switched AirPods or another headset into call mode, Daisy could lose the other side for the whole meeting. Daisy now listens to system sound directly — it asks macOS once for **System Audio Recording** (sound only, no screen), on its own window, not in the middle of a call.
- **If the way Daisy listens goes quiet, it tries the other one** in the same recording, without losing the timeline.
- **A shorter warning** when Daisy can't hear the other side: switch from Bluetooth headphones to wired ones or the device speakers.
- Changes of output device while a recording is starting are no longer missed.
