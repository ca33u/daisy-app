# Daisy 1.0.8.60

- **The plan is chosen first, then bought.** In Settings → AI the «Plan» row of the Daisy Subscription was two price buttons, and a click on either opened the payment page at once. Now the prices are a choice, «$7.99 a month» or «$59.99 a year», with the month picked to begin with, and a separate «Subscribe» button under it opens the payment page for the plan picked. Beside the button it says that the payment page opens in your browser
- **«Test summary» is shown on the Daisy Subscription only to a subscriber.** Without a subscription the button stood there greyed out, since a test would have spent one of the three free summaries; now the row isn’t drawn until the subscription is active. For your own provider nothing changed
- Not tried live: the new «Plan» row in the running app, where so far it was only drawn by a test, and with VoiceOver; a real payment; a summary written on the subscription
