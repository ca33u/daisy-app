# Daisy 1.0.8.60

- **«What’s New» tells about the Daisy Subscription.** After the update a window opens once by itself: summaries without an API key of your own, what else runs on the subscription, the two plans and one «Subscribe» button. It does not open by itself for an account that already has the subscription. Signed in and having agreed to send text, «Subscribe» opens the payment page and Settings → AI, where the payment is waited for; otherwise it takes you to Settings → AI with the plan you picked, and nothing is bought. Later: Help → «What’s New in Daisy»
- **The plan is chosen first, then bought.** In Settings → AI the «Plan» row of the Daisy Subscription was two price buttons, and a click on either opened the payment page at once. Now the prices are a choice, «$7.99 a month» or «$59.99 a year», with the month picked to begin with (or the plan picked in «What’s New»), and a separate «Subscribe» button under it opens the payment page for the plan picked. Beside the button it says that the payment page opens in your browser
- **«Test summary» is shown on the Daisy Subscription only to a subscriber.** Without a subscription the button stood there greyed out, since a test would have spent one of the three free summaries; now the row isn’t drawn until the subscription is active. For your own provider nothing changed
- **Dictation can be fixed by context. Off by default.** Settings → Dictation → «Fix dictation by context» asks a model to correct the words the recognizer misheard, spelling, grammar and punctuation, and to drop false starts, repeats and fillers, before the text is inserted. If the answer takes longer than 2.5 seconds, or doesn’t read as the same text, the dictation is inserted as recognized. The history keeps the text as recognized beside the corrected one: «Copy as recognized» in an entry’s menu. Hotkey dictation only; dictations shorter than 20 characters or longer than 1,200 are left as they are
- **What that switch sends, and where.** Audio never leaves the Mac. With the switch on, each dictation’s text and up to 40 entries of your vocabulary go to the summary provider in use, and for a provider that isn’t on this Mac only after you say yes to that very provider, named in the question. On the Daisy Subscription that is Daisy’s server, then Anthropic, and it needs an active subscription. Codex CLI, Cursor, OpenAI through a ChatGPT account and the Daisy local model are too slow and aren’t asked; with the local model chosen, the provider you had before it is, with a yes of its own. Turning the switch off withdraws every yes. Fields macOS marks as password fields are skipped. Apple Intelligence corrects on the Mac, but not Russian yet: such dictations are inserted as recognized
- **Daisy is now under the GNU AGPL, version 3 only.** The source moved to github.com/addicted-studio/daisy-app. A modified version that is distributed stays open under the same license, under a name and an icon of its own; versions published under Apache 2.0, from 22 May 2026 up to 1.0.8.59, remain available under it. About has a new section, «Legal notices». If you only use Daisy, nothing changes for you
- Not tried live: the «What’s New» window, the «Plan» row and the «Legal notices» section in the running app, where so far they were only drawn by tests, and none of it with VoiceOver; a real payment; a summary written on the subscription; «Fix dictation by context» on a real request to any provider, so how long it adds is not measured yet, nor were the switch’s question, its captions, «Copy as recognized» and the password-field skip seen at work
