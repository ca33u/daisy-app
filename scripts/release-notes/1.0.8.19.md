# Daisy 1.0.8.19

- **A meeting knows its project.** The summary of a meeting in a project now reads the project's notes and the summaries of its last few meetings — and of meetings with the same tag — so it can tell who is who and which thread the meeting continues. Notes and the switch are in Settings → Projects, behind the link icon of a project.
- **Meetings name themselves.** After the summary, "Meeting 2026-10-03 14:20" becomes a short title of what it was about. A title from your calendar, or one you typed, is never changed.
- **Rename in the list.** Right-click a recording in the Library → Rename, and type over its title in place.
- **Transcription shows how far it is.** An imported file now has a percentage in its row and a progress bar on its page, instead of "3 of 3 min" from the first second.
- **Codex is found again** after the ChatGPT app moved its helper inside the bundle.
- **Settings no longer quit** when their tabs were put back into the toolbar twice.
- **The import window is the active one** after a drop from Finder, so its Model and Language menus are readable.
- **Sections in Settings open by a click anywhere on their row**, not only on the arrow.
