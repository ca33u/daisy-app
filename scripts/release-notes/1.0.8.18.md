# Daisy 1.0.8.18

- **Next steps go where you are writing.** In a meeting's summary, "Insert into Mail" (or whatever app you were in) puts one step, or all of them, at the cursor of the app you used before Daisy — ⌘⇧V for the follow-up.
- **Send a Step.** Each next step on its own — to Notion, or to any destination in Connections, with who, by when and the lines it came from. A project can name its trackers (GitHub, Linear, Jira) in Settings → Projects, and a step opens there as a new issue, filled in.
- **Mail the recap.** The envelope in the summary's header writes to the meeting's attendees: the decisions and next steps, with who and by when.
- **Share Quote…** Select lines in a transcript and right-click: the quote with its time and speaker, and the same stretch of the recording as audio if you want it.
- **Questions carried from iPhone.** A question you set aside for the next meeting on the phone shows at the top of that meeting's prep as "Open from last time".
- **The compact menu bar opens under its icon** — it is a real menu now.
- **Dictation writes "ё" again** instead of dropping the word's middle.
- **Settings → Transcription** no longer asks you to download a model that is already there.
- **Calls in Bluetooth headphones:** when the other side is quiet at the start of a call, Daisy keeps listening the usual way instead of switching to one that can stay silent with Bluetooth, and comes back if it did switch and heard nothing.
