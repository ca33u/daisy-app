# Daisy 1.0.7.69

- **Merge speakers who got split in two.** When the same person comes back after a pause and lands as a second speaker, open the speaker's menu and pick "Same person as…" — both labels collapse into one name everywhere in the transcript. "Split" undoes it.
- **Export your dictionary.** The Vocabulary tab now writes every term and correction to a text file in the same format bulk import reads, so you can back it up, edit it in bulk, or carry it to another Mac.
- **Everything you can click now says so.** Sidebar rows, buttons, Library chips and card headers light up under the pointer. Card headers also open and close from anywhere along the header, not just the chevron.
- **One row of filters in the Library.** All, Recordings, Notes and your projects sit in a single row, and the tag filter stays put when you switch between them instead of quietly clearing itself. A filter that matches nothing now explains why rather than showing an empty page.
- **Fixed: deleting a multi-selection could remove recordings you couldn't see.** Selecting several rows and then narrowing the list by tag left Delete acting on the original selection, including the rows the filter had hidden. Bulk actions now only ever touch what's on screen.
- **"Voice" is now "My style"** in the sidebar — it was never about the microphone.
