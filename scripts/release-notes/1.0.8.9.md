# Daisy 1.0.8.9

- **Audio diagnostics during a recording show what the recording hears.** The microphone and other-side meters used to read "silence" while a recording ran, which looked like a dead microphone; they now show the recording's own levels.

Also in this build, from 1.0.8.8: no more gaps in the other side's transcript when speech and a repetitive chorus share a stretch; the widget is ready for the next recording the moment you press Stop.
