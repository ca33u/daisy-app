# Daisy 1.0.8.52

- **A muted microphone no longer stops a meeting recording.** When your microphone stayed silent for ten seconds — a mute switch on the headset, the input muted in the system, a closed lid over the Mac’s own microphone — Daisy paused the whole recording, the other participants included. Now, in a meeting recorded with system audio, the recording carries on and Daisy tells you no more than once per recording, for example «Mic is silent, still recording the other side», or, when it can’t tell a mute from a microphone that isn’t working, «Daisy can’t hear you» and «No sound is reaching Daisy from the microphone. The recording continues». Muting inside the meeting app normally didn’t cause this. Voice notes and meetings recorded from the microphone alone pause as before, and so does a recording whose microphone stops sending sound altogether, or disappears with no other microphone to switch to
