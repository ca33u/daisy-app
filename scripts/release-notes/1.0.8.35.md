# Daisy 1.0.8.35

- **The Daisy key.** Your voice-note key is now the Daisy key. Tap it for a note, as before. Hold it and talk: «note …» saves a note (add «to project Alpha» to file it), «shorter», «translate to English», «write a reply …» do it over the selected text or at the cursor with your summary provider, and a silent hold over a selection rewrites it in your voice. Whatever you say is kept in Dictation → History first, so nothing is lost. A result that can't go straight into a field opens a card next to the mouse with Copy, Insert, Show changes and Undo. Holding needs Accessibility.
- **The Daisy key in a meeting.** A tap starts a side note (a plaque shows while it's open; it ends by itself after a minute of quiet); a hold with words marks a moment with your comment, which goes with the moment instead of into the transcript. «Commands during a meeting» in Settings → Recording lets a hold run commands there too.
- **After a screenshot,** hold the Daisy key within five seconds and what you say is added to the screenshot's note.
- **Your own templates.** My style now has Follow-ups and Meeting notes. Make a follow-up template from one to three of your letters (names are taken out on your Mac) or a notes template — six ready ones, your own sections, or read from a note you like. Meetings get one by folder, a word in the title, or a default; the card switches between the template and the standard summary, every point keeps its timecode, and an empty section says «Not discussed». Pick the language of the notes or the letter per meeting, edit the letter in the card, and Copy asks while a [?] is left.
- **The widget while Daisy works:** white-to-lavender petals that turn while it understands, grow while it writes and flash once when it's done.
- **«Help make Daisy better»** is asked in the first-run setup and on Home instead of a popup; «Not Now» asks once more a few days later.
- **Settings → Summary → Allowed to send to the cloud** lists what you allowed and lets you take it back.
- **Models come from Daisy's own mirror** (models.mydaisy.io), each file checked before use, with Hugging Face as the fallback — and About → Models and licenses credits the people who made them.
