# Daisy 1.0.7.66

- **Live dictation preview actually shows your words.** Turning on "Live preview while dictating" was, until now, exactly what turned it off: the streaming engine it enables never fed the caption pill. It does now, and the pill closes the moment you release the key instead of flashing back over the paste.
- **Nothing is lost when the final pass comes back empty.** A finishing pass that produced no text used to wipe the transcript you already had — and, if you keep audio only until it's transcribed, the recording went with it. Both are fixed: no transcript, no deletion, ever.
- **Your first-run settings survive the second launch.** The dictation hotkey (Fn), automatic summaries and your audio-retention choice were being re-decided on every start and quietly reverted after the first restart. They're now saved when they're chosen.
- **Long meetings stay responsive.** Speaker separation was re-analysing the entire meeting on the main thread every 15 seconds — past the twenty-minute mark the interface barely moved. It now processes only what's new, in the background. Screen-text extraction after Stop moved off the main thread too: no more minute-long freeze at the end of a meeting with screenshots.
- **Releasing the dictation key always stops the recording.** Letting go while Daisy was still loading its model left the microphone open with no way to stop it, and the next press answered "already recording".
- **Daisy tells you when something is off instead of writing it to a log.** When there's no room on the disk it says the meeting is being recorded without audio — and the recording itself now remembers why, instead of showing an empty player weeks later. It says which microphone it's actually using when your chosen one isn't connected, says when a shortcut is already taken by another app, and stops calling a failed auto-paste a success: if Accessibility access is missing, it says so and offers the setting.
- **Crash recovery works again after the first recording of the day.** An internal flag was never cleared, which quietly disabled recovery of interrupted recordings and unfinished sessions for the rest of the run.
- **Honest engine reporting.** If macOS has no Apple speech model for your dictation language — or hasn't downloaded it yet — Settings now says which of the two it is, instead of silently running Whisper. "Models ready" no longer reports success when only some models arrived.
- **Export Logs goes straight to Downloads.** No save dialog, and the report now includes what Apple's speech engine offers for your language.
- **Removed a toggle that did nothing.** "Use the invite's headcount" asked you to weigh a trade-off while both settings produced identical results — the speaker-separation engine ignores the hint.
- **A microphone that drops out no longer takes the meeting with it.** If the mic can't be restarted after your Mac wakes up, the recording stays paused instead of failing — Resume or stop and save, your choice. And when the mic stops on its own mid-meeting, Daisy pauses and says so instead of showing a live recording with a frozen clock.
- **Audio is never deleted while it's the only copy.** Retention skips recordings that have no transcript yet, "Delete all audio files" waits for a running transcription to finish, and a storage move that hits an error keeps the old folder in view instead of forgetting it.
- **Edits made while Daisy is finishing up are kept.** Retitling, tagging or filing a meeting during the minute after Stop used to be silently reverted by the final pass. Not any more.
- **A single bad stretch of audio no longer costs the whole transcript.** If one block fails to transcribe, Daisy keeps everything else and fills the gap from the live transcript. An unreadable archive part is reported rather than quietly skipped.
- **Fewer wrong verdicts.** A recording that rolled into a second file was reported as "incomplete"; the "meeting seems over" prompt fired every three minutes when live transcript was off; the clipboard could be restored before a slow app had pasted your dictation. All fixed.
- **Privacy filter placeholders never appear in your text.** Names and secrets are swapped for tokens before a transcript goes to a cloud model and restored when it comes back; the restore now tolerates the ways models reshape those tokens, polish passes never use irreversible redaction, and any leftover placeholder makes Daisy keep your original words. This also unblocks the transcript polisher on Russian, which had been dropping every chunk.
- **Lighter on your Mac.** The widget stops redrawing when nothing is moving, the speaker-separation model loads only when a meeting needs it, and both models are released when the system is critically short of memory.
