# Daisy 1.0.7.72

- **Back-to-back meetings no longer cut each other off.** When the next calendar meeting starts while you're still recording, Daisy asks on the widget — "Next meeting — switch recording?" — instead of silently ending the current session. Tap to switch; ignore to keep recording.
- **The "Stop & save?" question is smarter.** It now appears only when the meeting is past its scheduled end and *both* sides have gone quiet. If Daisy has lost the other side's audio, it no longer mistakes your listening for an empty room.
- **The other side comes back after the screen sleeps.** If system-audio capture died because the display went to sleep or the lid closed, Daisy now retries as soon as a display is back, tells you when it has the other side again, and keeps the recording's timeline intact.
- **Dictation never touches your clipboard.** When an app won't accept a direct insert (web apps, Electron), Daisy now types your words instead of pasting them — so whatever you had copied stays copied. The old clipboard route is available under Settings → Dictation → "Paste through the clipboard" for apps that autocomplete as you type.
- **Re-transcription can be stopped**, and it shows progress in minutes instead of a bare spinner.
- **First launch ends with model preparation.** Choose a speech model, let Daisy download and verify it, then record. Speech detection and speaker separation are optional and finish in the background; "Skip for now" hands you the app right away. A failed model can be downloaded again from Settings → Transcription, with the previous files kept for diagnostics.
- Recording waits for a model that is still loading instead of refusing; a model that just failed isn't reloaded on every dictation; model preparation steps aside while a recording is running.
